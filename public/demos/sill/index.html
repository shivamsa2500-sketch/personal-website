From order to room

  1. Chosen

    Order online or in the showroom. Within a day we confirm the wood, the finish and your delivery week.

  2. Then built

    Every piece is made to order in a run of twenty or fewer. Most are ready in three to five weeks.

  3. Then delivered

    Two of us carry it in, set it up where you want it and take every scrap of packaging away.
