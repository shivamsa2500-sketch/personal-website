Our story

About the café

A neighbourhood coffee house built on one simple idea: good coffee, served with care.

View menu

Who we are

Started small, still local

We opened with one espresso machine, a borrowed grinder and a short menu we could do properly. It became the place the neighbourhood meets before work.

Our coffee is simple on purpose: a sweet, balanced house blend for milk drinks, a brighter single origin for black coffee, and a decaf that gets the same attention as everything else.

How we make it

From bean to cup

  1. 01

    The green bean

    Coffee is chosen on the cupping table, tasted blind, and bought for sweetness and balance rather than for a story on the bag.

  2. 02

    The roast

    Small batches, developed slowly so the sugars caramelise without the smoke. Rested for a week before it reaches the hopper.

  3. 03

    The grind

    Ground to order, seconds before extraction. Burrs are cleaned nightly and the grinder is re-dialled with every bag.

  4. 04

    The pour

    Twenty-eight seconds, give or take. Milk steamed to a glossy paint. The cup is warm before the coffee touches it.

What we care about

The house rules

  • Dialled in daily

    Every morning starts with a shot we taste before the first customer walks in. Grind, dose and time change with the weather, so we adjust.

  • Room to linger

    Timber tables, soft green seating and plenty of daylight. Bring a laptop, a book, or a friend; nobody will hurry you out.

  • Made with care

    Pastries laminated the day before and baked at dawn. Syrups made in-house. Milk steamed to the texture each drink needs.

  • A neighbourhood place

    We know regulars by their order. The counter is where the morning news is traded and the weekend is planned.

Timeline

How we got here

  1. Year 1

    A counter and six stools

    One espresso machine, a borrowed grinder and a short menu we could do properly.

  2. Year 2

    The bakery joins

    Laminated pastry made in-house, croissants from 7 am until they sell out.

  3. Year 3

    Beans go home

    The house blend moves from the hopper to the shelf; retail bags and a ceramic cup follow.

  4. Today

    The slow morning

    A full room, a longer menu, and the same rule: nothing on the bar we would not drink ourselves.
