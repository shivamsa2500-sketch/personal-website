FAQ

Before you come

  • Do you take reservations?+

    No, we're walk-in only. Larger groups are welcome; message us ahead and we'll try to keep a table free.

  • Can I work from the café?+

    Yes. There is free Wi-Fi, plenty of power points along the window bench, and we don't mind if you stay a while.

  • Are dogs allowed?+

    Well-behaved dogs are welcome at the outdoor tables, and water bowls are kept by the door.

  • Is the café accessible?+

    Step-free entrance, an accessible bathroom, and tables at wheelchair height. Let us know if we can help with anything.

  • Do you cater for allergies?+

    Oat, almond and soy milk are available for every drink. Ask at the counter and we'll walk you through pastry ingredients.
