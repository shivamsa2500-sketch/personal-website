About Clearline

We'd rather show you the problem than tell you

Most of what goes wrong in a house's plumbing is invisible until it isn't. Our job is to make it visible early, explain it plainly, and fix it once.

How we work

Quote in writing

Open up as little as possible

Detection first, demolition last. We'd rather spend ten minutes finding a leak than an hour patching drywall.

Leave it tidy

Drop cloths, boot covers, and the site cleaner than we found it.
