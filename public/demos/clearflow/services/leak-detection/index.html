Leak detection & pipe repair

San Francisco's older housing stock hides a lot of galvanized supply pipe. When it fails it rarely announces itself at the point of failure — the water travels. We locate the source first, then repair the smallest section that actually solves it.

Get a quote for thisCall (415) 555-0142

What the visit covers

  • Acoustic and moisture-meter leak location
  • Targeted access rather than exploratory demolition
  • Copper and PEX repair, partial or full repipe
  • Post-repair pressure test

Signs you'd notice

  • A water bill that climbs with no change in use
  • The meter still moves with every fixture closed
  • Staining, bubbling paint, or a musty smell in one room

How a job runs

Request, estimate, repair

Three steps, in that order. The estimate sits between the diagnosis and the work so nothing starts before the price is agreed.

  1. 1

    Request service

    Call, or send the details through the quote form. We ask what the symptom is, where it is, and when it started.

  2. 2

    Receive an estimate

    We diagnose the cause on site and put a written price to the work before anything is dismantled.

  3. 3

    Complete the work

    The agreed work is carried out, tested under load, and the site is left clean. The invoice matches the estimate.
