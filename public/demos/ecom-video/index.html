FAQ

Straight answers.

Coffee or energy drink?+

Both. Cold-brewed coffee is the base; the lift is its natural caffeine plus B-vitamins. No added sugar.

Why does it pour like a stout?+

A nitrogen widget releases on opening. Tiny bubbles give the cascading head and creamy body.

How much caffeine?+

160 mg per can, about two espressos. Two cans a day at most.
