Before launch

Four steps from demo to a real product page.

    Verify the data

  2. 02

    Check the rules

    Review labelling, warning and claim requirements for every market you sell in.

  3. 03

    Replace the placeholders

    Update the formula block in the site's configuration file. Every page reads from it.

    Swap in approved artwork

    Replace the SVG sample label with your approved packaging files.
