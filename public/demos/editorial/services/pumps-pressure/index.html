Service 08

Pumps & water pressure

Low pressure has a dozen possible causes. We measure it at the source and at the fixtures, so any pump or valve we recommend is solving the real problem.

Signs to look for

  • Weak flow upstairs or at the furthest fixture
  • Pressure that drops when two taps run at once
  • A booster pump that is noisy or short-cycling
  • Showers that swing between hot and cold

What the visit covers

  1. 01

    Measure

    Record static and flow pressure at the source and fixtures.

  2. 02

    Identify

    Find whether the cause is supply, pipework, valve or pump.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Service, adjust or replace the component at fault.
