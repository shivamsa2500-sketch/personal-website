Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information analystsolution.com ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website, our YouTube channel and social media accounts, and the internal tools we use to run them.

Information we collect

  • Public comments and messages. When you comment on our YouTube videos or send us a message on our social media accounts, we see your public display name and what you wrote.
  • Channel statistics. We read overall numbers for our own channel, such as subscriber count, views and number of comments. These are totals, not information about individual viewers.
  • Information you send us. If you email us or fill in a form, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name and email address.

How we use it

  • To read and reply to comments and messages on our own videos and posts.
  • To prepare a daily summary of how our own channel is performing.
  • To answer questions you send us.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising profiles, or share it with anyone except the service providers that help us run our tools (listed below).

YouTube API Services

Our internal tools use YouTube API Services to read comments and statistics for our own channel and to post replies that we have reviewed and approved. By interacting with our YouTube channel, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Our tools only access the YouTube account that belongs to analystsolution.com. Access can be revoked at any time through Google's security settings page.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted services to run our tools, including Google (YouTube), Anthropic (AI assistance for drafting replies and content, which we review before anything is posted), n8n (workflow automation) and Railway (hosting). They process information only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep information

Comment and reply records are kept for up to 12 months so we don't reply to the same comment twice, then deleted. Channel statistics are kept as long as they are useful for tracking our growth. You can ask us to delete information about you at any time.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own comments on YouTube at any time.
  • You can email us to ask what information we hold about you, or to have it deleted.

Children

Our content is made for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
