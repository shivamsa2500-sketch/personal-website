02Services

Analytics is the core of what we do.

Four areas cover most of what agencies hand over. Everything else on this page is built on top of them.

Marketing analytics

Performance across every channel a client runs, pulled into one view and read properly — so the monthly conversation is about decisions, not screenshots.

  • Channel and campaign performance analysis
  • Budget efficiency and spend allocation
  • Period-over-period and cohort comparisons
  • Attribution sense-checks across sources

Advertising analytics

Where the ad budget is working, where it is leaking, and which levers are worth pulling next — across search, social and programmatic.

  • Campaign, ad set and creative breakdowns
  • ROAS, CPA and contribution analysis
  • Audience and placement performance
  • Creative fatigue and testing read-outs

Website & conversion analytics

What visitors actually do on the site, where the journey breaks, and what the drop-off is costing the client each month.

  • Traffic quality and landing page analysis
  • Funnel and drop-off mapping
  • Form, checkout and lead-flow diagnostics
  • Tracking audits and event setup review

Reporting & dashboards

The recurring work, handled. Monthly client reports and live dashboards that stay accurate without someone rebuilding them every cycle.

  • Monthly and quarterly client reporting
  • Live dashboards for agency and client views
  • Automated data collection and refresh
  • Templates standardised across your roster

Data analysis

Messy exports, mismatched platforms and half-cleaned spreadsheets turned into one reliable dataset you can actually report from.

Conversion analysis

A focused look at where users leave and which fix is worth doing first, sized by the revenue behind it.

Business analytics

KPIs beyond the campaign — retention, repeat rate, margin and lifetime value — for clients who ask what marketing is doing for the business.

Additional digital solutions

Secondary capabilities, usually taken on alongside an analytics engagement rather than on their own.

Website creation

Landing pages and small business sites, built to be measurable from day one.

Tracking & tagging setup

Analytics, events and conversion tracking implemented or repaired.

Data pipelines & automation

Scheduled pulls from ad platforms and sheets so reports build themselves.

Internal tools

Small custom tools for the workflows your team repeats every month.

03How we work

Four steps, and you're only involved in two of them.

01

Understand

A short brief on the client, the objective, the data available and what the deliverable needs to look like. Usually one call.

02

Analyse

We collect and clean the data, then work through it properly — looking for the patterns that change a decision, not the ones that fill a slide.

03

Deliver

Report, dashboard or recommendation set, in your format and your branding, with the findings written in plain language.

04

Support

Follow-up questions answered, dashboards kept running, and the next cycle scheduled before you have to ask.
