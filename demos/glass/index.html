How we handle it

From first call to water back on.

No mystery, no waiting around for a callback that never comes. Three steps, each with a person on the other end.

  1. 1

    Tell us what's happening

    Call or send a request. Describe what you see, hear or smell. A photo helps. We'll tell you what to switch off right away.

  2. 2

    Get a plan, not a mystery

    We confirm when we can be there and what the likely fix involves. If it's bigger than a quick repair, you'll know before we arrive.

  3. 3

    Fixed, tidied, explained

    We do the work, leave the space as we found it, and show you what was wrong so you can spot it earlier next time.
