The process

  1. Arrival
  2. Purify
  3. Correct
  4. Protect
  5. Reveal

0102030405

01/05 · Arrival

Rolled in as it is.

Road film, brake dust and car-wash swirls. We inspect every panel under studio light and photograph it before we touch it.

  • Photo check-in
  • Paint depth readings

02/05 · Purify

Foam, wash, decontaminate.

Snow foam, a two-bucket hand wash, iron fallout remover and clay. Everything bonded to the paint comes off safely.

  • pH-neutral foam
  • Iron + tar removal
  • Clay bar

03/05 · Correct

Polish out the swirls.

One to three machine polishing stages lift swirl marks and light scratches, panel by panel, until the clear coat reflects like glass.

  • Swirl removal
  • Up to 3 stages
  • Checked under light

04/05 · Protect

Lock it in with ceramic.

A hard ceramic coating bonds to the clear coat. Water beads and rolls off, and dirt lets go with a simple rinse.

  • Hydrophobic
  • UV protection
  • Easier washes

05/05 · Reveal

Better than the day you bought it.

A final walk-round under studio light, then your keys and a simple care guide. Most cars are ready in one to three days.
