No. 05 · From the farm

Grown on hills you can name

Most of our coffee comes from four growing regions in South India, where arabica grows under shade trees between 950 and 1,500 metres. We buy from estates we've visited, pay for the lots we cup best, and print the farm, altitude and process on every single-origin bag. A few coffees come from Ethiopia, Colombia and Brazil, chosen for flavours our hills don't produce.
