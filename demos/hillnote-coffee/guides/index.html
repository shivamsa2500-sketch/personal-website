No. 03 · Quick recipes

Quick recipes for every other brewer

  • Espresso

    Short, intense and syrupy. Needs a grinder you trust or beans we grind fine.

    Coffee
    18 g
    Water
    36 g out
    Temp
    93 °C
    Time
    25–30 s
    Grind
    Fine, like powdered sugar
    How to brew it

    Dose 18 g into the basket, level and tamp. Aim for 36 g of espresso in 25–30 seconds. Too fast and sour: grind finer. Too slow and bitter: grind coarser.

  • Moka pot

    Stovetop strength. Start with hot water so the coffee doesn't stew.

    Coffee
    16 g
    Water
    160 ml hot water
    Temp
    Just off the boil
    Time
    4–5 min
    Grind
    Fine-medium, like fine sand
    How to brew it

    Fill the base with hot water to just below the valve. Fill the basket level without pressing. Brew on medium-low heat with the lid open, and take it off the heat as soon as it starts to sputter.

  • AeroPress

    Quick, clean and travel-proof. Steep, stir, press.

    Coffee
    15 g
    Water
    230 ml
    Temp
    88–90 °C
    Time
    2:00
    Grind
    Medium-fine, like table salt
    How to brew it

    Add coffee and 230 ml water, stir 3 times, cap and steep until 1:30. Press slowly for 30 seconds. Top up with hot water if you like it longer.

  • South Indian filter

    A slow-drip decoction for milky, sweet filter coffee.

    Coffee
    20 g
    Water
    110 ml
    Temp
    95 °C
    Time
    15–20 min drip
    Grind
    Fine-medium, like fine sand
    How to brew it

    Add coffee to the upper chamber, set the press disc on top, and pour in water just off the boil. Close and leave it to drip. Mix 1 part decoction with 2–3 parts hot milk and sugar to taste.

    Shop coffee for South Indian filter →

  • Cold brew

    Steep overnight in the fridge. Smooth, low in acid, keeps for a week.

    Coffee
    100 g
    Water
    1 litre cold water
    Temp
    Fridge
    Time
    16–18 h
    Grind
    Extra coarse, like cracked peppercorns
    How to brew it

    Stir coffee into cold water in a jar, cover and refrigerate 16–18 hours. Strain through a paper filter. Dilute 1:1 with water or milk over ice.

No. 05 · Basics

Three habits that matter most

  1. 1

    Weigh it: 1 to 16.

    Use about 1 g of coffee for every 16 g of water. A kitchen scale beats any scoop, because a scoop of light roast weighs more than a scoop of dark.

  2. 2

    Hot, but off the boil.

    Aim for 90–96 °C. If you don't have a thermometer, boil the kettle and wait 30 seconds. Darker roasts like the lower end, lighter roasts the higher end.

  3. 3

    Fresh, then rested.

    Coffee needs a few days after roasting to settle, then it's at its best for about a month. Keep the bag sealed, away from light and heat, and out of the fridge.
