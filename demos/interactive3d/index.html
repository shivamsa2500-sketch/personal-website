How do I shut off the water in an emergency?

Find the main shutoff valve, usually where the supply line enters the house near the meter or in the basement. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If you can't find it, call us and we'll walk you through it.

Pricing is set by the real business. This demo leaves it blank rather than inventing a number.

Can you work on a tankless water heater?

Yes. Descaling, error-code diagnosis, replacement and new installation.
