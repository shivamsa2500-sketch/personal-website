Our story

A counter, six stools and one rule.

From bean to cup

Four small decisions, every cup.

The difference between fine and memorable is a handful of details, repeated all day.

  1. 01

    The green bean

    Coffee is chosen on the cupping table, tasted blind, and bought for sweetness and balance rather than for a story on the bag.

  2. 02

    The roast

    Small batches, developed slowly so the sugars caramelise without the smoke. Rested for a week before it reaches the hopper.

  3. 03

    The grind

    Ground to order, seconds before extraction. Burrs are cleaned nightly and the grinder is re-dialled with every bag.

  4. 04

    The pour

    Twenty-eight seconds, give or take. Milk steamed to a glossy paint. The cup is warm before the coffee touches it.
