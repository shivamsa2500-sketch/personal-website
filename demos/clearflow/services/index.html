How a job runs

Request, estimate, repair

Three steps, in that order. The estimate sits between the diagnosis and the work so nothing starts before the price is agreed.

  1. 1

    Request service

    Call, or send the details through the quote form. We ask what the symptom is, where it is, and when it started.

  2. 2

    Receive an estimate

    We diagnose the cause on site and put a written price to the work before anything is dismantled.

  3. 3

    Complete the work

    The agreed work is carried out, tested under load, and the site is left clean. The invoice matches the estimate.

Before you call

What to expect

  • Diagnosis before quoting. The point of the first visit is to establish what is actually wrong, because the visible symptom and the underlying cause are often in different places — particularly with leaks.

  • For a straightforward replacement, often yes. For anything behind a wall or below a floor, a number given over the phone is a guess, and a guess that later changes is worse than no number at all.

  • Some work in San Francisco requires a permit, particularly where it involves the sewer lateral or significant re-piping. A real business would configure its permit handling here; this demo does not make a claim either way.

  • This demo has emergency service switched off in its configuration, so the site does not advertise it. That switch exists deliberately: a site should never claim a response capability the business has not confirmed it can meet.
