About

A company built around the estimate

Clearflow is a fictional brand, but the process it describes is a real one: diagnose the fault, write down the price, then do the work.

Mission

Take the guesswork out of it

The worst part of a plumbing problem is rarely the plumbing. It is not knowing whether the number you have been given bears any relation to the work, and not being able to check.

The fix is unglamorous: find the cause before quoting, write the price down, and explain what was found in terms that make the decision yours rather than ours.

That is the philosophy this demonstration site is built to express — in the structure of the pages as much as the copy.

In practice

Communication, workmanship, care

  • You hear what we found

    A photo or a camera recording of the actual fault, not a verdict delivered from under the sink.

  • The price is written before the work

    Agreed in advance. If the job changes once a wall is open, it stops and gets re-quoted.

  • The repair is sized to the fault

    A failed section replaced rather than a whole run, unless the run is the problem.

  • The site is left clean

    Floors covered, old units taken away, the cabinet drier than we found it.

Values

Four things we hold to

The rules that decide what happens when a job turns out to be bigger than it looked.

  • Diagnose, then quote

    A price given before the cause is known is a guess. We find the fault first and put a number to the actual work.

  • Open the smallest hole

    Exploratory demolition is a last resort. Locating a leak properly usually means repairing one section rather than a wall.

  • Say what it will cost

    The estimate is written down before the work starts, so the invoice holds no surprises.

  • Leave it clean

    The old unit goes with us, the floor gets covered, and the cabinet is left drier than we found it.
